Embracing the Inner Parent and Child

Transpersonal Psychological Strategies for Growth and Healing

Starting from basic concepts such as personality, self and Essence, it gradually introduces the work of growth from the perspective of the inner child, inner parent and transpersonal.

Content introduction

The first half of the book establishes a conceptual framework for understanding people and discusses the body, emotions, thoughts, and different inner states; the second half turns to practice, explaining how to identify injured inner spaces and how to establish connections with supportive inner resources.

This book focuses on

  • Distinguish between the different levels of personality, self and Essence.
  • Recognize the wounded inner child and how it manifests in current relationships.
  • Understand the significance of the inner parent as a supportive resource.

Who should read it?

Suitable for readers who want to further understand the "inner child" and "inner parent" in Essence Therapy.
