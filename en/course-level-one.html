Essence Therapy Level I

Start with physical sensations and understand the core method of Essence Therapy.

Essence Therapy · Getting Started

Course at a Glance

Starting from the integrated perspective of transpersonal psychology, using body feelings as the entrance, you can learn to identify the inner space and understand the inner child's work and inner parent resources.

Who is suitable to study

Learners who hope to get to know Essence Therapy systematically; partners who are engaged in psychological counseling, education or other helping work, can also establish a common method language from the first level.

What to learn in the course

  • Understand the transpersonal psychology perspective and integrative approach to Essence Therapy.
  • Starting from physical sensations, identify current emotional reactions and different inner spaces.
  • Understand the working methods of the inner child and learn to connect and anchor the inner parent resources.
  • Through on-site demonstrations and group exercises, understand the core processes of Essence Therapy.

learning style

Michel Claeys On-site leadership, combining method teaching, demonstration and group practice; learning around body feelings, inner child and inner parent resources.

learning objectives

Establish a preliminary ability to identify body feelings and inner space, and understand Essence Therapy how to move from experience to work.

What can I learn next?

After the first level, you can continue to read the original work of Michel Claeys based on the learning objectives, and understand the arrangements for the second level, dreamwork or practice supervision.
