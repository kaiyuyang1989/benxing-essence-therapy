OUR PHILOSOPHY

Our Teaching Philosophy

Team principles of listening, practice, boundaries and continuous learning.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Respect experience and respect professional boundaries

The course uses theoretical lectures, method demonstrations and group exercises to help learners build experience. Everyone’s experience and tolerance are different, and leaders need to respect their rhythm and boundaries.

In the course, theoretical lectures, method demonstrations and group exercises support each other; continuous practice and review are still required after class.

IN PRACTICE

Four principles of practice

01 / LISTEN

Listening into work

Pay attention to the client's current physical feelings and expressions, and avoid hasty explanations.

02 / PRACTICE

Pay attention to practice and integration

Through demonstration, practice and post-class review, learners are supported to apply the methods to real life.

03 / RESPECT

Respect personal rhythms and boundaries

In group and individual work, adjust the depth of exploration according to the wishes and status of the participants.

04 / TOGETHER

Continuous learning and supervision

Professional abilities rely on long-term accumulation of reading, practice, feedback and supervision.
