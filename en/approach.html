THE APPROACH

Essence Therapy: Method and Principles

From listening and embodied awareness to inner resources and action, explore Michel Claeys's path of inner work.

WHAT IT IS

Use body feelings as the entrance to work

Recurring emotions and reactions often require deeper understanding in the present experience.

Essence Therapy is a counseling approach developed by Michel Claeys. It begins by listening, then attends to present bodily feelings, distinguishes wounded and resourceful inner spaces, and supports new choices in daily life.

What is introduced here is the learning entrance. To understand the method, scope of application and practical operation in depth, please read the original work of Michel Claeys and study under a leader with corresponding qualifications.

THE INNER WORK

Seven Steps of Inner Work

From listening to integration, Michel outlines a path that can be revisited as needed.

01

LISTENING

Listen to the concern

Listen to what is troubling the client and notice recurring feelings and reactions.

02

PRESENCE

Return to the body

Move from explanation to awareness: what is felt in the body here and now?

03

OPENING

Make room for feeling

Allow the feeling to be noticed and accepted, without rushing to suppress or explain it.

04

EXPLORATION

Explore its origins

When appropriate, follow present feelings toward earlier experience; regression is not required in every session.

05

SHIFTING PERSPECTIVE

Step back from the wounded part

Recognize that a wounded inner space is part of one's experience, not the whole self.

06

INNER RESOURCES

Respond from inner resources

Reconnect with a resourceful inner space, such as the ‘inner parent,’ and respond differently to old emotions.

07

INTEGRATION

Anchor and integrate

Strengthen that resourceful experience, clarify needs and choices, and bring concrete action into daily life.

These steps describe a possible path, not a fixed script. Work follows the client's pace and may return to listening, feeling or inner resources.

KEY IDEAS

Understand several concepts in reading methods

These words point to concrete experiences at work. Get to know them first, then read the full method discussion.

body feeling＋

Paying attention to body reactions such as breathing, tension, or relaxation in the moment provides a perceptible gateway to understanding emotions.

inner space＋

Michel Claeys Use this statement to describe people’s inner experiences in different states. Recognizing these states can help you understand your immediate reactions.

resource space＋

In addition to the experience of injury, there may also be feelings of stability, support, and strength within; the work will focus on reconnecting with these.

Integrate changes＋

Bring the understanding gained in consultation or class back to relationships and daily life, and continue practicing with specific choices.

SEVEN PRINCIPLES

Seven Guiding Principles

The steps describe a process of inner work; the principles guide the perspective throughout counseling.

  1. 01self-responsibilityStart where you can choose.
  2. 02Totally presentReturn to the feeling of the here and now.
  3. 03age regressionListen to where the hurtful experience is coming from.
  4. 04deidentificationDon't regard an injured state as your whole self.
  5. 05Transform energyRespond to old feelings with new relationships.
  6. 06anchor coreConnect with the powerful inner space.
  7. 07Integrate changesClarify your next steps and bring the experience back to life.
