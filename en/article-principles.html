Michel Claeys《Seven Principles of Essence Therapy》《What is Essence Therapy》

The practical context of the seven principles

Michel Claeys emphasized in the public article: The focus of Essence Therapy is to help clients experience and integrate a set of inner working abilities. It’s about responsibility, physical feelings in the moment, space for hurt and resources, and bringing change back into life. Memorizing the seven items does not mean that you have mastered the working methods.

Differences between early and 2024 statements

The "Seven Principles of Essence Therapy" published earlier are written in sequence as "taking responsibility, anchoring, returning to the present, amplifying, de-identifying, transforming, and integrating change." The 2024 update discussion uses the above "self-responsibility, total presence, age regression, removal of identification, transformation of energy, anchoring core, integration of change". The two versions have a common core, but the focus and order are different. The two sets of expressions present Michel Claeys’s different emphasis on work steps; when learning operations, it is still necessary to understand the connection between each link.
